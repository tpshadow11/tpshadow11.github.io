Shazam: Audio Fingerprinting

A simplified Shazam: spectrogram peaks become a "constellation map," peak pairs become hashes, and a clip is identified by matching hashes against a database. It still finds the right song with half the clip silenced or buried in heavy noise.

Lab 02 Week 6, Fall 2026

Signals | Localization

Acoustic Positioning System

Six speakers loop unique Gold codes and one microphone records the mix. Cross-correlation separates the speakers, arrival-time differences become a linear system, and least squares solves for the microphone position, landing within 0.4 cm on a real recording.

Lab 03 Week 8, Fall 2026
SVD
Data Science | Speech

Voice Recognition with PCA

A spoken-word classifier built on principal component analysis. Each recording is aligned, enveloped, and projected onto three principal components from the SVD; a nearest-centroid rule then classifies all 48 held-out recordings correctly.

Lab 04 Week 10, Fall 2026
2D convolution
Signals | Imaging

Image Deconvolution

Motion blur modeled as convolution with a point-spread function. The direct inverse amplifies noise into static, while Fourier-domain Tikhonov regularization recovers the image; a no-reference score also identifies the unknown blur direction.

Medical Imaging: MRI & CT

MRI scanners measure Fourier coefficients and CT scanners measure line integrals. I designed a 25% k-space mask (center disk plus spiral) reconstructed with compressed sensing, and chose the fewest CT views that meet explicit error and dose limits.
