Who I am

About
Me

I’m a UC Berkeley undergraduate studying Applied Mathematics, Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, and Data Science. I’m especially interested in optimization and statistical modeling, and in how to test whether a model’s assumptions hold up in practice. I’m looking for early-career roles in robotics software and applied machine learning, especially work that involves building simulation and evaluation tools, debugging robot behavior, or integrating software with hardware. I’m also interested in solutions engineering roles where I can help teams deploy and troubleshoot robotics systems.

I like figuring out why robots fail. At Algoverse AI, I studied whether a robot could use a short explanation of a failed attempt to recover on its next try. To test that fairly, I needed the simulator to reproduce the same failure each time. I traced inconsistent replays to how randomness was handled between runs and fixed the issue so we could compare recovery strategies under the same conditions.

I serve as Internal Vice President at Underwater Robotics at Berkeley, where I work with the electrical and software teams. I design circuits for our autonomous underwater vehicle’s power distribution and safety boards and work on the behavior trees that control how it operates. As VP, I track progress across the club, coordinate recruitment and social events, and lead outreach to companies. I’m also a Test Organizer on the Operations & Activities team at the Berkeley Math Tournament, where I help run competitions for students from around the world.

Outside of class, I enjoy photography, hiking, and playing guitar. I listen to US-UK pop, rock, V-pop, K-pop, and J-pop, and I recently started posting music covers on YouTube again.
