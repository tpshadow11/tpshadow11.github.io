Who I am

About
Me

I'm an Applied Mathematics (B.A.) and Data Science (B.A.) double major at UC Berkeley. I focus on applied modeling, data science, machine learning, and robotics. I also have hardware experience in circuits and embedded systems.

Besides the technical field of engineering and software, I'm also passionate about mathematics and its applications such as optimization and statistical modeling. It shapes my critical thinking and problem-solving skills through analysis as I have a curiosity for understanding the underlying principles.

Outside of my academics, I also do photography, hiking, and participating in clubs. Two roles I'm proud of: Test Organizer on the Operations & Activities team at Berkeley Math Tournament, and Internal Vice President (Electrical Team) at Underwater Robotics at Berkeley.

My Story

Jan – Nov 2021 · Phu Nhuan High School, Vietnam

A first spark: the STEM smart home

Built an automated smart house for a STEM competition — Arduino Uno, sensors, and a hand-built model out of mica board and dental sticks. It was my first real taste of engineering, but the project didn't land the way I'd hoped, and I stepped away from it for a while.

Aug 2022 – Jun 2023 · Westminster High School, California

Senior year, after moving from Vietnam

Finished high school at Westminster, earning a LEO Award in Mathematics for the 2022–2023 year.

Aug 2023 · Orange Coast College

Started as an Art major

Enrolled aiming to become a 2D animator.

Dec 2023 – Aug 2024 · Bober Tea and Coffee

Working the counter

Took a barista/cashier job — opening and closing shifts, drinks and pastries — while figuring out what to actually study.

Spring 2024

Switched to Pure Mathematics

One semester in, I realized animation wasn't the path. I declared Pure Math and got drawn to the idea of becoming an instructor — which sent me looking for a way to actually try teaching before committing to it.

Sep 2024 – May 2025 · UCI CalTeach & Orange Coast College

Testing what teaching actually feels like

Through UCI's CalTeach dual-enrollment program, I designed 5-E lesson plans and taught 3rd-grade math and Algebra 1, logging 50+ hours of fieldwork at Davis Elementary Magnet School and McGarvin Intermediate School. At the same time, I took a front-desk Math Tutor role at OCC — one-on-one sessions and small study groups — to see the same question from another angle.

April 2025

Accepted to UC Berkeley

Admitted as a Pure Mathematics major.

Fall 2025 · UC Berkeley

Applied Math, Data Science, and a way back to engineering

Pure math felt too broad without something to point it at, so I switched to Applied Mathematics with a Data Science concentration and added a Data Science + EECS minor. That's where I found my way back to robotics and engineering — the interest I'd first felt in high school, this time with the tools to actually build something.

2025 – Present

Building

Since then: leading electrical work at Underwater Robotics at Berkeley, organizing for Berkeley Math Tournament, running vision-language-action robotics research at Algoverse AI, and hands-on projects spanning a treble-boost guitar pedal, a procedurally generated dungeon game, and an autonomous shopping robot at CalHacks.
