Language feedback for robot policies: evaluating self-correction in Vision-Language-Action models

Built a GPU evaluation harness on Modal for the 7B-parameter OpenVLA robot policy on the LIBERO manipulation benchmark. It captures failed episodes, replays each one deterministically, and runs a matched-pair test of whether simulator-grounded language feedback helps the robot recover. I also benchmarked five VLA architectures and LoRA-fine-tuned OpenVLA on reflection data.
